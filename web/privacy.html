Privacy Policy

Version . Operated by (contact: ).

FresherPilot is a private, invite-only job-search assistant. This policy explains what personal data it stores, why, who it is shared with, and your rights under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act). "We" means the operator named above; "you" means a member with an account.

1. What we collect and why

DataWhy (purpose)
Account: username, email and Google account ID (if you use Google sign-in), a salted password hash (if you use a password), when you accepted these termsTo sign you in and keep your data separate from other members
Profile and resume: name, contact details, education, skills, experience, the answers you save for application forms, your resume fileTo match jobs to you, fill application forms and write messages when you ask it to
Your API keys (Groq, Apify, Gmail app password, Serper)To run the services you connect, with your own accounts. Stored encrypted; never shown to anyone, including the operator, in readable form through the app
Job-search data: job listings found for you, AI results, jobs you opened, applications you tracked, messages you sent and their logTo show your job list, dashboard and history
Session cookie and the browser extension's tokenTo keep you signed in (essential; no tracking or advertising cookies)
Basic request data (IP address in short-lived memory for sign-in rate limiting)Security: to block password guessing

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or build profiles of you for anyone else.

2. Data about other people (recruiters and companies)

To help you reach out, the app looks up publicly available business contact details of recruiters and companies (for example from company websites and public search results) and keeps them in a shared cache. It only uses information that was made public for professional contact. Anyone whose details appear can ask us to remove them using the contact above, and we will delete them and stop collecting them.

3. Who data is shared with (processors)

Only what each step needs is sent, and only when that feature runs:

  • Groq (AI): job descriptions and a summary of your profile/skills, to judge fit and draft answers or messages.
  • Apify (with your key): search keywords and cities, to fetch job listings.
  • Google: sign-in (Google Identity Services); Gmail, if you connect your own Gmail to send emails you approve.
  • Serper / DuckDuckGo: company names, to find public contact pages.
  • Cloudflare: carries the encrypted connection between your browser and this app.
  • Job sites and employers: when you apply or send a message, the details you choose are sent to them.

These providers have their own privacy policies. Some process data outside India.

4. Where it is stored and how it is protected

Data is stored on the operator's computer in India, in a separate folder per member. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes; API keys are encrypted; the connection is HTTPS through Cloudflare; the app is reachable only through that tunnel. No system is perfectly secure: if a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

5. How long we keep it

Account, profile and history: until you delete your account (or the operator removes it). Job listings are dropped automatically after 3 weeks. Deleting your account removes your folder at once; the app keeps no separate copies.

6. Your rights

  • Access: Settings → "Download my data" gives you everything stored for your account.
  • Correction: edit your profile, resume and settings in the app at any time.
  • Erasure / withdraw consent: Settings → "Delete my account" deletes your account and data at once.
  • Grievance: write to . We aim to reply within 7 days and resolve within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
  • Nominate: you may name a person to exercise these rights for you in case of death or incapacity, by writing to us.

7. Age

FresherPilot is only for people aged 18 or older.

8. Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the version above changes and the app asks you to accept it again.
